Accessibility statement

Last updated: 21 August 2026 · שחר הפקות AI · V0.1

We aim to meet Israeli Standard 5568 and WCAG 2.0 Level AA, under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013. This is not legal advice and is not an external accessibility audit certificate.

1. Adjustments on this site

  • Semantic structure, keyboard navigation, and a visible focus mark.
  • A skip-to-content link and a persistent accessibility widget (icon) for larger text, high contrast, grayscale, highlighted links, readable font, paused animation, and a large cursor.
  • Preferences are stored on your device.
